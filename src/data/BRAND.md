# FullDum — brand note

**Always styled FullDum.** One word, capital F and D. Never “Full Dumb.” Never “Full Dum” in the logo or the name. “On dum” (lowercase, two words) is only for the cooking method, as in the biryani line.

FullDum is a merch brand for food and desi life: chai, coffee, biryani, the house, and Gulf-life humour. Witty and warm. It does not take itself too seriously, and it does not punch down.

## Voice

- Short, kitchen-table lines. Dry, not loud.
- Affectionate about food and the wait: the pour, the dum, the first cup, the bag of gifts.
- English first, with the odd Hindi, Tamil, or Arabic line when it is natural and correctly written.
- No kitchen-brand names, no “full dumb” jokes, no religious verses, no flags, no airline, bank, or cooker marks.

A useful test: would you say it to a cousin over chai? If it needs a punchline about anyone’s accent, religion, or employer, it is not FullDum.

## Palette

| Role | Name | Hex |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary ink | Charcoal | `#2A2724` |
| Warm accent | Turmeric | `#E0A624` |
| Punch accent | Brick red | `#B33C28` |
| Paper (support only) | Paper | `#F6EFE4` |

Charcoal carries type and line art. Turmeric is the reserved accent (rules, steam, a stub of a ticket, the underline under *Dum* in the wordmark). Brick red is used sparingly — a tag, a single word, a lid that needs heat. Paper is the ground and the knock-out colour on a charcoal fill. Do not add navy, coffee-brown, or neon.
